Solid walls, no dead ends. The circular boundary is impassable. A head that steers into it slides along the edge instead of stopping or dying. Conquest and kill-rush: cut a rival's trail or win a head-on and you absorb their whole territory and get a 1.5x speed burst for 5 seconds, enough to chase down a second kill. The victim respawns with a small starting patch on empty ground. Only a cut into your own trail releases land back to neutral. Speed also scales with size: +1 cell/s per 5% of the map owned.
