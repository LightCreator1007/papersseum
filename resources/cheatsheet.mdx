## Symbols

| Symbol | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| $s$, $s'$ | a state, and the next state |
| $a$ | an action |
| $r$, $R$ | a reward |
| $\gamma$ | discount factor, between 0 and 1 |
| $G_t$ | the return from time $t$ |
| $\pi(a \mid s)$ | probability the policy picks $a$ in state $s$ |
| $V(s)$ | value of a state |
| $Q(s, a)$ | value of taking $a$ in state $s$ |
| $\alpha$ | step size (learning rate) |
| $\varepsilon$ | exploration probability |

## Averages and bandits

Expected value of a quantity that takes value $x_i$ with probability $p_i$:

$$
E[X] = \sum_i p_i x_i.
$$

Running average, after a new observation $x$ (this is the pattern behind nearly every update in the course):

$$
\text{new estimate} = \text{old estimate} + \alpha\,(x - \text{old estimate}).
$$

## Returns and MDPs

$$
G_t = R_{t+1} + \gamma R_{t+2} + \gamma^2 R_{t+3} + \cdots
$$

## Bellman equations

$$
V^\pi(s) = \sum_a \pi(a \mid s) \sum_{s'} P(s' \mid s, a)\,\big[r + \gamma V^\pi(s')\big]
$$

$$
V^*(s) = \max_a \sum_{s'} P(s' \mid s, a)\,\big[r + \gamma V^*(s')\big]
$$

## Learning from experience

Monte Carlo: move the estimate toward the return of the whole episode.

$$
V(s) \leftarrow V(s) + \alpha\,\big(G - V(s)\big)
$$

TD(0): move it toward the reward plus the estimate of the next state.

$$
V(s) \leftarrow V(s) + \alpha\,\big(r + \gamma V(s') - V(s)\big)
$$

SARSA (on-policy):

$$
Q(s,a) \leftarrow Q(s,a) + \alpha\,\big(r + \gamma Q(s',a') - Q(s,a)\big)
$$

Q-learning (off-policy):

$$
Q(s,a) \leftarrow Q(s,a) + \alpha\,\big(r + \gamma \max_{a'} Q(s',a') - Q(s,a)\big)
$$

## Gradient descent and networks

Step against the slope of the error $E$ with respect to a weight $w$:

$$
w \leftarrow w - \alpha \,\frac{dE}{dw}.
$$

A neuron: a weighted sum, then a bend (an activation function such as the ReLU, which is $\max(0, z)$):

$$
\text{output} = f\big(w_1 x_1 + w_2 x_2 + \cdots + b\big).
$$

## DQN

Target and loss:

$$
\text{target} = r + \gamma \max_{a'} Q_{\text{target net}}(s', a'), \qquad \text{loss} = \big(Q(s,a) - \text{target}\big)^2.
$$

## Policy gradients

Softmax:

$$
\pi(a) = \frac{e^{\theta_a}}{\sum_j e^{\theta_j}}.
$$

For a softmax, the slope of $\ln \pi(a)$ with respect to the scores is "one-hot minus probabilities". The update after taking action $a$ with advantage $A$:

$$
\theta \leftarrow \theta + \alpha\, A\, \nabla \ln \pi(a).
$$

## Elo

$$
E_A = \frac{1}{1 + 10^{(R_B - R_A)/400}}, \qquad R_A \leftarrow R_A + K\,(S_A - E_A).
$$
